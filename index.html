Research Experience

Volumetric 3D Printing for the Rapid Production of Artificial Organs 01/2024 – Present
  • Assembled a volumetric printing platform with precision mechanical and optical performance for rapid artificial organ production.
  • Calibrated light sources, adjusted optical systems, and optimized component compatibility.
  • Developed and optimized a MATLAB slicer, significantly increasing slicing speed and production efficiency.
  • Determined optimal printing material formulations to meet biocompatibility and mechanical requirements.
Multiphysics Modeling of Frontal Curing-Enabled Additive Manufacturing for Carbon Fiber/Thermoset Composites 08/2023 – 01/2024
  • Simulated frontal curing 3D printing for carbon fiber/epoxy composites using COMSOL.
  • Developed models exploring frontal curing effects across different configurations.
  • Analyzed carbon fiber content's impact on curing process, frontal temperature, and degree of cure.
  • Investigated how continuous and discontinuous carbon fibers influence curing parameters.
