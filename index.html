Research Experience

Volumetric 3D printing used for the rapid production of hybrid structure 03/2024 – Present
  • Assembled a volumetric printing platform, ensuring precision in mechanical and optical performance for the rapid production of artificial organs.
  • Calibrating the light source of the UV projector, adjusting optical systems, and optimizing the compatibility between different components.
  • Developed and optimized MATLAB slicer code for the project, significantly increasing slicing speed and overall production efficiency.
  • Conducted various mechanical tests on the manufactured hybrid hydrogel, including tensile, shear, and peel tests; Manufactured the parts needed for the setup using a variety of 3D printing methods, including FDM, SLA, DLP, VPP, etc.
Thermal analysis of 3D GPU–memory architectures with boron nitride interposer 05/2025 – 12/2025
  • Developed a 3D thermal model of GPU–HBM heterogeneous integration using COMSOL Multiphysics to simulate heat transfer across multi-layer packaging structures.
  • Evaluated thermal performance of hexagonal boron nitride interposers compared with conventional silicon through temperature distribution and hotspot analysis.
  • Performed parametric studies on interposer thickness, material properties, and boundary conditions to identify optimal thermal design ranges.
  • Analyzed heat flux and junction temperature to support thermal management strategies for high-power computing devices.
Multiphysics modeling of additive manufacturing for carbon fiber/thermoset composites 08/2023 – 03/2024
  • Simulated frontal curing 3D printing for carbon fiber/epoxy composites using COMSOL Multiphysics.
  • Developed different models to explore frontal curing's effects across different configurations in application.
  • Analyzed the impact of carbon fiber content on the curing process and parameters, including frontal temperature and degree of cure.
Hopping Light Vat Photopolymerization for Multi-scale Structure Fabrication 01/2022 – 12/2022
  • Assembled the Vat Photopolymerization platform and built the digital model of the platform.
  • Carried out the practical application experiment. Designed and manufactured an eggbeater structure and tested for its hydrophobic properties.
  • Used different 3D printing methods to make the structural parts of the experimental platform.
